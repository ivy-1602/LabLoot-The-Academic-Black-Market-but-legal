## 🍷 The Real Problem

It's 11 PM. Lab journal due tomorrow at 10 AM. You haven't started. Your friend's friend has a "standard journal" but won't share. Google gives theory. YouTube gives tutorials. Nobody gives you the actual, print-ready, SPPU-formatted, viva-questions-included journal.

**Enter LabLoot.**

---

## What This Is ❔

An **E-Commerce website** (SE Sem 3 project) that sells ready-made lab packs, theory notes, and complete bundles for SPPU Engineering students (2019 Pattern - Computer & AI-ML).

**What's in a pack:**
- 📄 Complete programs (tested, commented, professor-approved format)
- 🖥️ Real output screenshots (actual terminal outputs, not Photoshop)
- 📋 Index pages (properly numbered, SPPU format)
- 🎓 Cover pages (print, fill roll number, submit)
- ❓ Viva Q&A (25-35 questions with model answers)
- 📝 Write-up guides (theory, algorithm, flowchart)

**The workflow:** Download → Print → Get signed → Submit → Pass okay

**Built for:** SPPU's SE/TE/BE students (CS & AI-ML, Sems 3-8)  
**Vibe:** Honest student humor meets professional e-commerce

## ✨ E-Commerce Features (Assignment Requirements)

### Core Features
- 🏪 **Product Catalog** - Browse by branch, semester, category
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** - Find specific labs or notes
- 🛒 **Shopping Cart** - Add multiple products, manage quantities
- 💰 **Pricing System** - Individual packs (₹799-₹1,099) and bundles (₹2,999-₹3,999)
- 📦 **Product Categories** - Notes, Lab Packs, Bundles
- 👤 **User Accounts** - Sign up, login, order history
- 💳 **Checkout Flow** - Cart → Details → Payment
- 📥 **Digital Downloads** - Instant ZIP delivery after payment

### Additional Features (Beyond Requirements)
- 🎯 **Breaking News Ticker** - Live updates, creates urgency
- ⭐ **Student Testimonials** - Social proof, builds trust
- 📊 **Bundle Deals** - Upselling strategy
- 🎨 **Brand Identity** - "Academic Black Market" positioning
- 📱 **Responsive Design** - Mobile-first approach
